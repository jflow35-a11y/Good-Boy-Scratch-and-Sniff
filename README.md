# Privacy Policy  
**Good Boy! Scratch and Sniff**

Last updated: January 2026

---

## Overview

Good Boy! Scratch and Sniff is designed to respect your privacy. The app does not require an account, does not collect personal information directly, and works primarily offline.

Some limited data may be collected automatically by Apple or by our advertising provider to support ads and in-app purchases.

---

## Information We Do Not Collect

We do **not** collect or store:
- Your name
- Your email address
- Your phone number
- Your precise location
- Your contacts
- Any login or account information
- Any user-generated content
- Any personally identifiable information

We do not run our own servers and do not track users across apps or websites.

---

## Advertising

The free version of the app displays advertisements.

Ads are provided by a third-party advertising service (such as Google AdMob). This service may automatically collect limited, non-personal information to display ads and measure performance. This information may include:
- Device type
- Approximate (non-precise) location
- Advertising identifiers
- App interaction data

This data is collected and handled by the advertising provider according to their own privacy policies.

Purchasing the one-time unlock removes ads entirely.

---

## In-App Purchases

The app offers a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase to unlock the Continuous Walk and remove ads.

All purchases are processed securely by Apple using Apple’s In-App Purchase system. We do not receive or store any payment, billing, or credit card information.

The app supports Apple’s “Restore Purchases” feature, allowing users to restore purchased content on devices using the same Apple ID.

---

## Analytics

We do not use custom analytics or tracking systems to monitor individual users.

Apple may collect anonymous usage statistics as part of standard App Store operations.

---

## Data Storage

Any progress, preferences, or game data are stored locally on your device only. This data is not transmitted to us or to any external server.

Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

---

## Children’s Privacy

Good Boy! Scratch and Sniff does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Any advertisements shown in the app are handled by the advertising provider in accordance with applicable child privacy regulations and platform requirements.

---

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be made available within the app or on the App Store listing.

---

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you may contact the developer through the App Store support link associated with the app.
